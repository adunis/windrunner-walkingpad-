## Downloads

- **WalkingPad.exe**: the Windows app. Just run it. If SmartScreen warns, choose *More info → Run anyway*.
- **Windrunner.zip**: the WoW addon. Unzip it into `World of Warcraft\_retail_\Interface\AddOns` (or `_forever_`, `_classic_era_`…), so you get `AddOns\Windrunner\Windrunner.toc`.

## New in Windrunner 3.4

- **Your route on the world map (M)**:
  - It's drawn live while a session runs.
  - **Show on world map** in the Sessions window shows the route of any saved session.
  - It works with Blizzard's map and with **OpenMap**'s stitched map, which gets a *Windrunner route* switch in its menu.
  - `/wr route` turns it on or off.

## Windrunner 3.3

- **Sessions window** (Sessions button or `/wr sessions`):
  - Every session, with your character's route drawn on the zone map.
  - Distance per zone, dungeon, raid, battleground or delve.
